# Community standards

TheraSim aims to keep project discussions respectful, focused, and safe for
people with varied professional, cultural, and lived experiences.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful and constructive; critique ideas and implementation, not
  people.
- Describe evidence and uncertainty honestly. Do not present simulated
  content, project evaluations, or rubrics as clinical advice, validated
  measures, or evidence of treatment effectiveness.
- Use synthetic examples only. Do not disclose personal, patient, client,
  credential, or other sensitive information.
- Respect the repository's mixed-license boundary and applicable third-party
  terms.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of
sensitive data, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Contact the repository maintainer through a private channel available from the
GitHub repository. Do not post private information or unpatched security
details in public issues. Reports will be reviewed as the maintainer's time
allows; confidentiality and a particular response time cannot be guaranteed.

The maintainer may edit, remove, or close contributions that violate these
standards or put people or private data at risk.
